# Brand & visual theme

See also [`brand/README.md`](../../brand/README.md) (assets + token table).

## SHALL

1. The system SHALL use the shared SurveyDesk theme tokens from `brand/README.md` on public web and both operator mobile apps.
2. The system SHALL use `brand/surveydesk-full.jpg` as the full brand image (illustration + SurveyDesk + tagline) on marketing/home/splash surfaces.
3. The system SHALL use `brand/surveydesk-logo.png` as the logo-only mark with a transparent background (app icon, compact nav, favicon, header, mobile splash/sidebar). Opaque `surveydesk-logo.jpg` SHALL NOT be used in chrome.
4. Primary actions (e.g. Submit, Open survey) SHALL use `color.primary` `#0878F8`.
5. Rating stars (`rating_1_5`) SHALL use `color.rating` `#F8B000`.

## SHALL NOT

1. The system SHALL NOT invent a different product logo or replace SurveyDesk wordmark colors.
2. The system SHALL NOT use unrelated purple-on-white gradient “AI default” themes in place of this palette.
